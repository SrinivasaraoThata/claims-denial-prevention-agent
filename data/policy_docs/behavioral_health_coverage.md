# Behavioral Health Coverage

## Scope
Applies to:
- 90837 - Psychotherapy, 60 minutes

## Requirement by plan
Prior authorization is not required for outpatient psychotherapy under any
of the four plans (PLAN001 PPO Gold, PLAN002 HMO Standard, PLAN003 EPO
Bronze, PLAN004 POS Silver). Behavioral health services are covered at
parity with medical and surgical benefits under all plans, consistent with
mental health parity requirements.

## Visit frequency
No plan imposes a visit cap on outpatient psychotherapy. Sessions billed
more frequently than twice per week for the same member may be flagged for
documentation review but are not automatically denied.

## Common denial reasons
- Rendering provider is out-of-network and the plan does not offer an
  out-of-network behavioral health benefit (PLAN002 HMO Standard only)
- Session billed on a date where the member's coverage was not active
- Duplicate billing for the same member and date of service
