# Prior Authorization: Surgical Procedures

## Scope
Applies to the following surgical procedures:
- 29881 - Knee arthroscopy with meniscectomy
- 27447 - Total knee replacement
- 19120 - Breast biopsy, excisional

## Requirement by plan
- PLAN001 (PPO Gold): Prior authorization required for all three procedures.
  Elective procedures (29881, 27447) require documented conservative
  treatment history of at least six weeks before approval.
- PLAN002 (HMO Standard): Prior authorization required. All surgical
  procedures must be performed by or referred through the member's assigned
  primary care provider.
- PLAN003 (EPO Bronze): Prior authorization required for 29881 and 27447.
  19120 (breast biopsy) does not require prior authorization when ordered
  following an abnormal imaging or clinical exam finding.
- PLAN004 (POS Silver): Prior authorization required for all three
  procedures at both in-network and out-of-network facilities.

## Clinical criteria for elective knee procedures (29881, 27447)
Authorization is approved when documentation shows:
- Failed conservative treatment (physical therapy, anti-inflammatory
  medication, or injection) for a minimum of six weeks
- Imaging (X-ray or MRI) confirming the structural finding requiring surgery
- Functional impairment affecting activities of daily living

## Common denial reasons
- No prior authorization on file at the time of claim submission
- Conservative treatment history not documented for elective knee procedures
- Procedure performed at a facility outside the authorized network
