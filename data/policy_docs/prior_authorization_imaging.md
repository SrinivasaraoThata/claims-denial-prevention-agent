# Prior Authorization: Advanced Imaging

## Scope
Applies to advanced imaging services, including:
- 70551 - MRI brain without contrast
- 70553 - MRI brain with and without contrast

## Requirement by plan
- PLAN001 (PPO Gold): Prior authorization is required for all advanced imaging
  services regardless of place of service.
- PLAN002 (HMO Standard): Prior authorization is required and must be requested
  by the referring in-network provider. Out-of-network imaging is not covered.
- PLAN003 (EPO Bronze): Prior authorization is required. Requests submitted
  without supporting clinical notes (symptoms, duration, prior conservative
  treatment) will be pended for additional information rather than denied
  outright.
- PLAN004 (POS Silver): Prior authorization is required for in-network
  providers and for out-of-network providers alike.

## Clinical criteria
Authorization for brain MRI (70551, 70553) is approved when at least one of
the following is documented:
- New or worsening neurological symptoms (e.g. focal deficit, seizure,
  significant headache change) present for more than two weeks
- Follow-up imaging for a previously identified structural abnormality
- Pre-surgical planning for a confirmed neurological or ENT procedure

## Common denial reasons
- Claim submitted without an approved prior authorization number
- Authorization requested after the date of service (retroactive requests are
  reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed approval)
- Imaging performed at an out-of-network facility without an approved
  network exception
