# Preventive and Routine Care

## Scope
Applies to:
- 99213 - Office visit, established patient, low complexity
- 99214 - Office visit, established patient, moderate complexity
- 93000 - Electrocardiogram, routine
- 71046 - Chest X-ray, 2 views
- 36415 - Venipuncture, routine blood draw

## Requirement by plan
None of the four plans (PLAN001 PPO Gold, PLAN002 HMO Standard, PLAN003 EPO
Bronze, PLAN004 POS Silver) require prior authorization for these services
when ordered by an in-network provider. These are treated as routine
outpatient office and diagnostic services.

## Network considerations
- PLAN002 (HMO Standard) does not cover these services when rendered by an
  out-of-network provider, except in a documented emergency.
- PLAN001, PLAN003, and PLAN004 cover out-of-network office visits and
  diagnostics at a reduced benefit level; no authorization is required at
  either network tier.

## Common denial reasons
- Service rendered by an out-of-network provider under PLAN002 (HMO
  Standard) without an emergency or network-adequacy exception
- Member not eligible on the date of service
- Duplicate claim for the same service, provider, and date of service
