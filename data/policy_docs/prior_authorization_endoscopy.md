# Prior Authorization: Endoscopy Procedures

## Scope
Applies to:
- 45378 - Diagnostic colonoscopy
- 43239 - Upper GI endoscopy with biopsy

## Requirement by plan
- PLAN001 (PPO Gold): Prior authorization is not required for a routine
  screening colonoscopy (45378) for members age 45 and older. Prior
  authorization is required when the procedure is diagnostic (ordered due to
  symptoms) rather than a routine screening.
- PLAN002 (HMO Standard): Prior authorization is required for both 45378 and
  43239 regardless of screening or diagnostic intent.
- PLAN003 (EPO Bronze): Prior authorization is required for 43239. 45378 does
  not require prior authorization.
- PLAN004 (POS Silver): Prior authorization is required for both procedures
  when performed at an out-of-network facility; not required in-network.

## Common denial reasons
- Diagnostic intent billed without a prior authorization on file (PLAN001,
  PLAN002)
- Screening colonoscopy billed for a member under the plan's minimum age
  without a documented family history or symptom-based indication
- Repeat procedure within the plan's minimum interval (typically 24 months)
  without new clinical indication
