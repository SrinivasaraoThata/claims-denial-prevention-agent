# Physical Therapy: Visit Limits and Authorization

## Scope
Applies to:
- 97110 - Therapeutic exercise, physical therapy

## Visit limits and authorization by plan
- PLAN001 (PPO Gold): First 12 visits per calendar year do not require prior
  authorization. Visits 13 and beyond require authorization based on a
  progress note showing functional improvement.
- PLAN002 (HMO Standard): First 6 visits per calendar year do not require
  prior authorization. Authorization required beyond that, along with a
  referral from the member's primary care provider.
- PLAN003 (EPO Bronze): First 10 visits per calendar year do not require
  prior authorization. Authorization required beyond that.
- PLAN004 (POS Silver): First 8 visits per calendar year do not require
  prior authorization. Authorization required beyond that.

## Common denial reasons
- Visit count for the calendar year exceeds the plan's threshold without an
  authorization on file
- No progress note submitted with the authorization request for continued
  visits
- Referral missing for HMO Standard members beyond the initial visit
  allowance
